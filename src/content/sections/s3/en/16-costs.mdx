The S3 bill has three parts: the space you use, the <Term id="request">requests</Term> and the <Term id="data-transfer-out" /> to the internet. When you are starting out, it tends to be quite small.

Pick an example or type in your own numbers.

<S3Island name="costCalculator" />

You may have read that S3 gives you 5 GB free for 12 months. That changed: since July 15, 2025, new accounts get credits to spend on many services, S3 included. If you choose the <Term id="free-plan" />, you pay nothing for up to 6 months, or until the credits run out, and the calculator shows the current amounts.

For peace of mind, create an alert in AWS Budgets. It emails you when the bill goes past the amount you choose.
