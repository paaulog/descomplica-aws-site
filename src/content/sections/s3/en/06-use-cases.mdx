Almost every app you use stores files somewhere. These are the most common uses of S3:

- **User files:** profile photos, attachments and documents uploaded to an app.
- **Backups:** copies of a database or of a company's computers.
- **Static websites:** the HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images of a website.
- **Videos:** the lessons of an online course platform, for example.
- **Data for analysis:** logs and huge files that feed reports.
