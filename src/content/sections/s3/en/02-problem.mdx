Imagine your app keeps profile photos on its own server's disk. It works fine at first. Until the day it does not.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The disk fills up">
    Every new user brings more photos. One day the space runs out, and uploads stop working.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The disk breaks">
    Disks wear out. If one breaks and there is no copy, the photos go with it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Traffic explodes">
    When the app takes off, the same server has to deliver thousands of files at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="And the bill?">
    A bigger disk, a spare server, someone to look after the copies. It all costs money, even when
    idle.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

You might be thinking: "just buy a bigger disk". That works, until next time.
