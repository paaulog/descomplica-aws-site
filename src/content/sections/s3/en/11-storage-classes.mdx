Not every file is opened every day. That is why S3 has <Term id="storage-class">storage classes</Term>, each with one price to store and another to read.

The ones you will see the most:

- **S3 Standard:** the default, for things used all the time.
- **S3 Intelligent-Tiering:** moves each object between tiers on its own, based on usage. Great when you do not know the access pattern.
- **S3 Standard-IA:** for things read rarely that still need to be ready right away. IA stands for _Infrequent Access_.
- **S3 Glacier:** the archive family, with three classes (Instant Retrieval, Flexible Retrieval and Deep Archive). The cheapest to store and the most expensive to read.

Move the control and watch the recommendation and the cost change.

The rule of thumb: the less you access it, the cheaper it is to store and the pricier it is to read.
