Everything in S3 revolves around four pieces. The <Term id="bucket" /> holds the files, each file becomes an <Term id="object" /> and each object has a <Term id="key" />, its full name.

The fourth piece is the <Term id="region">Region</Term>: the place in the world where the bucket lives, such as `us-east-1` (N. Virginia) or `sa-east-1` (São Paulo). You pick the Region when you create the bucket and cannot change it later.

Upload a file and see what S3 stores along with it. That extra information is the <Term id="metadata" />.

You might be thinking: "what about folders?". In S3 they do not really exist. Type a key and see how the console turns the slashes into <Term id="prefix">prefixes</Term> that look like folders.
