Remember the luggage storage? By default, only whoever holds the key can open the locker. In S3, that key is your account's credentials, and <Term id="iam" /> decides what each of them can do.

There are three common ways to open the door:

- **Private (the default):** only people with credentials and permission get in.
- **Public:** you turn off <Term id="block-public-access" /> and write a <Term id="bucket-policy" /> that lets everyone read.
- **Temporary link:** a <Term id="presigned-url" /> works only until it expires. From the console it lasts up to 12 hours; from code or the command line, up to 7 days.

Switch the access type and see who can read the file.

When in doubt, go with the presigned URL. It shares one specific file, for a short time, without opening the whole bucket.
