<Analogy title="The luggage storage that never fills up">
Think of a <Term id="bucket" /> as a locker with a name nobody else can use. You can store as many items in it as you want, and AWS will never tell you it is full.

- The **locker** is the bucket.
- Each **stored item** is an object.
- The item's **tag** is the key: the name that says exactly which item it is.

You do not need to know which shelf the item is on. You hand the tag over at the counter, and the attendant brings the item.

</Analogy>
